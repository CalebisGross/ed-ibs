## 6 LED drive: high-frequency PWM and lit-period current boost
[S] Gemini BOM: Mean Well LRS-200-24 (24 V constant-voltage PSU) and a DRV8833 (motor driver) on the controller; no constant-current LED driver part is named; the manual refers to an 'LED driver PCB' and its current limit
[S] TI SNVA605 / TIDA-01415 (LM3409 + shunt FET): shunt-FET PWM dimming above 30 kHz with sub-microsecond edges; TPS92641 shunt drive delay 29-100 ns

## 8 how bright is the step streak if it is not blanked
[C] exposure per metre of wall = 1 / beam speed. A spoke is crossed twice per beat by the radial sweep; the streak once.
[C] 8.00 Hz, swing +: where the streak is off-spoke (>0.25 x 2.69 m), its exposure is 47% (median) / 46-49% of the spoke at the same radius
